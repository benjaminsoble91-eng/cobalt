IconSwap

com.mymculture.iconswap

About

Rearranging the Home Screen by dragging is fiddly. IconSwap lets you swap two apps instantly: enter jiggle mode, tap one icon, then tap another, and they trade places.

How to use

  1. Long-press the Home Screen to enter jiggle (edit) mode.
  2. Tap the first app you want to move.
  3. Tap the app you want it to trade places with.
  4. They swap. Repeat as needed, then press Done.

Settings

  • Turn IconSwap on or off without uninstalling.
  • Haptic feedback on or off.
  • Highlight on the first app you tap, on or off.
  • Hint message on or off.
