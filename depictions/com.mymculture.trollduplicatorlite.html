TrollDuplicator Lite

com.mymculture.trollduplicatorlite

About

Make copies of your apps using the jailbreak — no TrollStore needed. Each copy is a normal app with its own name, its own data, and its own spot on the Home Screen.

How it works

  1. Open TD Lite and tap the app you want to copy. Every app is listed, including TrollStore and jailbreak apps.
  2. Give the copy a name and choose how many copies to make.
  3. TD Lite gives it a new bundle ID, re-signs it with ldid, and installs it through TrollStore Lite.
  4. The copy shows up on your Home Screen. Delete it there, or from TD Lite.

Good to know

  • Needs TrollStore Lite and ldid (Sileo installs both for you). No AppSync Unified.
  • App Store apps must be decrypted first, or the copy won't open.
  • Widgets, extensions and Watch apps aren't copied.
  • Copies only run while the device is jailbroken.
  • Works on rootful (Odyssey, Taurine, Chimera) and rootless (Dopamine) jailbreaks.
